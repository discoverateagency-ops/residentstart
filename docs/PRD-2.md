## PM summary

Phase 1 works: contracts, investor flow, buyer flow, payments and the tour mechanics are live at residentstart.biz.id. What is wrong is how it **looks**. Two things make the site read as a prototype instead of a property company:

1. **The virtual tour shows flat synthetic renders**, not a house. The viewer (Pannellum) does the same job as the viewer on a real-estate 360 tour; the difference is the pictures. Our four panoramas are geometric colour blocks generated by a script (`gen_pano.py`) as placeholders. A real tour uses photographs, and adds a thumbnail strip, a floor-plan minimap and walk arrows on the floor. **Fix: use the 15 real show-unit photos supplied on Nov 13 as a 13-stop photo walkthrough (each photo projected at its true field of view in the same viewer, with arrows, thumbnails and a floor-plan minimap), delivered ready-made in `handoff-2/`. Full 360° files can replace any stop later without code changes.**
2. **The landing page is built around a cartoon illustration and one project card.** Pinhome and Rumah123 are built around a search bar, dense property cards with real photos, price in "Juta/Miliar", KT/KM/LT/LB specs, location chips and trust rows. **Fix: rebuild the landing as a buyer-facing property portal with real photos, and move investor content to its own page.**

This revision adds: a four-item navigation (Home · For Buyer · For Investor · Blog), separate pages for buyers (funded homes) and investors (projects open for funding, with investor login), a blog with six readable articles, five seeded projects instead of one, photo sets for every project, and two photographic panorama sets.

**What the revision must prove on stage:** a judge opens the landing page and sees a property portal; clicks a home, opens the tour, and is standing in a real-looking room; clicks For Investor and sees a different project open for funding, with the same quality tour; clicks Blog and reads an article. Nothing in the contracts or payment flow changes.

**What we do not do in this revision:** no Bahasa UI toggle, no search backend (the search bar filters the seeded list client-side), no map integration beyond a static image, no new contract methods, no change to agents except project context, no Matterport-style dollhouse (that needs a 3D scan).

**Cut order if time runs out:** R7 blog article bodies → R6 investor page polish → R4 landing extras (location chips, testimonials). Never cut R1 (tour images), R2 (viewer), R3 (landing core) or R8 (README/submission).

### 2. Tour v2 (R2)

Keep Pannellum (already in the codebase; it supports partial panoramas natively via `haov`/`vaov`/`vOffset`) and port the behaviour of `tour-test.html` into the Next.js tour component:

- Top bar: "‹ Back · {project} · {stop name}"; green `rs-green-700` at 94 %.
- Viewer with a dark-green background outside the photo; `hfov` = 92 % of `haov`, min 45 %, max 100 %.
- **Arrows:** 44 px green discs with a white arrow glyph, tooltip label from `links[].text`; links with `pitch ≤ −30` render as "back" arrows (glyph rotated down).
- **Thumbnail strip** (bottom, 108 px): one tile per stop, active tile outlined white, auto-scrolls to the active stop.
- **Floor-plan minimap** (bottom-left, 220 px / 160 px mobile): tabs "Ground floor / Upper floor", pins from `rooms[].map`, current pin highlighted, click a pin to jump; the tab switches automatically when the stop is on the other floor.
- First-load hint "Drag to look around · tap the green arrows to walk through", fades after 3.5 s; fullscreen control; gyroscope off by default (partial panoramas feel wrong with gyro).
- Credit line bottom-right: "Photos: Residentstart show unit · illustrative, specs may differ".
- `?debug=1`: clicking prints yaw/pitch to the console for adjusting arrows.
- Config loaded from `/tours/<set>/rooms.json`; the `rooms` table is replaced by `tour_sets` holding that JSON.

Photo Sphere Viewer is no longer required; mention it only in "Later".

## UI specification (new and changed screens)

Global rules from `docs/PRD.md` still apply (tokens, pills, formatting). New rules for this revision:

- **Photos everywhere.** No vector illustrations of houses anywhere on the site. Every card, hero and gallery uses a photograph.
- **White page background** (`#FFFFFF`) for landing, /buy, /invest, /blog; `rs-bg` only for dashboards. Green is for the header bar, CTAs, prices and pills, not for large fills.
- **Headline sizes** down: H1 36/44 desktop, 28/34 mobile. Body 15–16 px. Pinhome/Rumah123 are information-dense; big friendly type reads as a toy.
- **Price format for cards**: `Rp 2,85 Miliar` / `Rp 950 Juta` (one decimal, comma), with a second line `Cicilan mulai Rp 27,6 Juta/bln (15 thn)`. Detail pages show the full rupiah figure.
- **Spec row** on cards: `KT 3 · KM 2 · LT 120 m² · LB 150 m²`, plus certificate `SHM` as a small grey tag. (KT = kamar tidur, KM = kamar mandi, LT = luas tanah, LB = luas bangunan.) Keep the English UI elsewhere; these abbreviations are what Indonesian buyers scan for.
- **Badges** top-left on card images: `Siap huni` (completed), `Dalam pembangunan` (in_construction), `Sisa 2 unit`, `Tur 360°` (bottom-right icon badge when a tour exists).
