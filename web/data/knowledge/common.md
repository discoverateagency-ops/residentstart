## About Residentstart
- Residentstart is a property developer in Jakarta that funds its own residential projects through a DAO on the NEAR blockchain. Tagline: "property crowdfunding".
- Two sides: investors fund a project by buying shares in the Residentstart DAO (projects with status "funding", listed on the For Investor page); home buyers purchase finished or funded homes in cash or by installment financed by the DAO pool (For Buyer page).
- This platform is a hackathon demo for the NEAR Hackathon (Nov 2026). It runs on NEAR testnet with synthetic data. No real money, no real property sale, no real investment is happening.
- Team: founders Septianus Angga Wicaksono (CEO) and Nindar Feriko (COO). The same team built SentFund, a NEAR testnet payroll demo.

## How money moves (demo)
- Investors deposit NEAR (testnet) into the dao contract and receive shares in a chosen funding project. 1 share = 10 NEAR; the remainder is refunded in the same transaction.
- Buyers pay in Indonesian rupiah. In this demo the rupiah payment is simulated (Virtual Account or QRIS on a simulator page). Each paid installment is hashed and recorded on the vault contract on NEAR, so investors can verify repayment progress of the whole DAO pool on NearBlocks.
- Contracts: dao.residentstart.testnet (shares, voting) and vault.residentstart.testnet (loans, repayment receipts). Explorer: https://testnet.nearblocks.io.
